4 · Assured0Established and effective: controls recorded, and the plan delivered and signed off - or the risk formally accepted.
3 · Managed0Established with gaps: controls recorded and every gap has an owned, dated action - all on time.
2 · Vulnerable0Deteriorating: the plan is overdue, or actions are missing an owner or a date.
1 · Uncontrolled2Not established: no controls recorded, no plan and no formal acceptance, or the risk is not yet scored.
