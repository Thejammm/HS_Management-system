H&S control maturity - what each level means
LevelRisksWhat it means
4 · Assured0Established and effective: controls recorded, and the plan delivered and signed off - or the risk formally accepted.
3 · Managed22Established with gaps: controls recorded and every gap has an owned, dated action - all on time.
2 · Vulnerable20Deteriorating: the plan is overdue, or actions are missing an owner or a date.
1 · Uncontrolled3Not established: no controls recorded, no plan and no formal acceptance, or the risk is not yet scored.
Graded on recorded facts, not opinion. The response must match the size of the risk: Critical and High risks must be 4 · Assured or 3 · Managed on time, and never assured on acceptance alone; Medium risks must not be left 1 · Uncontrolled.
